Hey! I'm Jake, a Chicago-based creative technologist. My background is in VFX and compositing. I studied VFX at DePaul, with minors in animation and computer science, and that work is still a big part of what I do.

I started with video editing and got hooked on compositing. Nuke is still my pixel sandbox: I like figuring out why an image isn't working and getting it to feel right. I also like writing small tools in Python that help me and a team get to the creative work faster. That problem-solving habit has gradually pulled me into projection, AV, and interactive systems too.

Recently, I've worked with a team on the TouchDesigner-based Project Projection installation for DePaul's Jarvis Challenge, handled projection mapping and playback for the short film Shed, and supported the on-site AV build and system testing for ESPN Sports Nirvana with Visual Feeder.

What I enjoy most is the stretch where visuals, software, hardware, and the actual room have to agree. I like building things, troubleshooting the parts that don't quite work, and working with the people around me to get them running. I'm interested in more work across creative technology, experiential media, projection and AV, and VFX/compositing.
