# Application Positioning

Use this as background when tailoring resumes, cover letters, project ordering, or site copy. Public website pages should stay focused and should not become a full career-search document unless Jake asks for that.

## Nuke Compositor / Junior Compositor

Lead with:

- Nuke-first compositing
- Realism and believable image integration
- Shot problem-solving
- Color and light matching
- Cleanup, roto, tracking, projection, and CG integration
- Feedback iteration and junior-level growth mindset
- Strong examples: The Lagoon, Motopia, 60s Vista, Khans, ETEE once finalized

Avoid:

- Overloading the pitch with every adjacent interest
- Making AI or tooling sound like the main artistic claim
- Hiding WIP status where shots are still in progress

## Roto / Paint / Prep

Lead with:

- Paint cleanup under practical shot constraints
- Roto and holdout discipline
- Stabilization and tracking to support invisible fixes
- Long-frame-range consistency
- Foreground occlusion, motion blur, and flashing-light challenges
- Strong examples: `echo010` from The Lagoon, LOSERS, ETEE, 60s Vista, Stadium/Kids Running exercises where useful

Frame as:

- Comfortable with patient detail work
- Interested in making invisible fixes hold under motion
- Able to break a messy shot into solvable layers

## Pipeline Or Technical Assistant

Lead with:

- Computer science minor
- Python and Nuke API/tooling
- Workflow debugging
- Linux if confirmed for the role
- Perforce helper and file/path discipline
- Portable `.nuke` startup/menu systems
- Render/version safety and artist-facing reliability
- Pipeline-minded problem solving visible in both tools and shot work

Frame as:

- An artist who understands technical friction because he hits it in real shot work
- Someone who likes turning repetitive pain points into small reliable tools
- Junior enough to learn studio standards, but already thinking in systems

## Creative Tech / Interactive Installation

Lead with:

- TouchDesigner, projection mapping, real-time visuals, and installation storytelling where confirmed
- Sensors/camera input if confirmed
- Real-time systems and creative prototyping
- The connection between visual design and technical systems

Frame as:

- A hybrid creative technologist with VFX taste and technical curiosity
- Someone who can think through both visual impact and implementation details

## Motion Graphics / 3D

Lead with:

- Cinema 4D, After Effects, Maya, Unreal Engine, Substance Painter, and motion/3D work shown on the site
- Real-time cinematic work such as Sunshine
- 3D motion graphics pieces such as Abstract Shapes and Honey
- Lookdev/modeling pieces such as Alarm Clock

Frame as:

- Secondary but real breadth
- Stronger for roles that value generalist motion/design support than for pure compositor applications

## Post-Production / Assistant Editor / Finishing

Lead with:

- Resolve/Fusion background from early editing/compositing work
- Reel/site organization and project finishing instincts
- Interest in color, finishing, Flame, and post workflows
- Detail-oriented cleanup and image continuity

Frame as:

- A technically curious post-production generalist with a compositor's eye
- Someone who can support image workflows, organization, and revisions

## Creative AI / Generative Media

Lead with only when relevant:

- AI-assisted workflow prototyping
- Nuke CopyCat experimentation where appropriate
- ComfyUI only after Jake confirms concrete work
- Codex/OpenAI as a support tool for organization, scripting, and prototyping

Frame carefully:

- AI assists workflow, exploration, and prototyping.
- Jake's artistic judgment, shot analysis, and finishing decisions remain the center of the work.
- Do not make AI sound like a replacement for compositing skill.
