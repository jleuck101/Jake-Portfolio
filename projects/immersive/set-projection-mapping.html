Set Projection Mapping — Short Film

On-set projection mapping for a short film directed by Stephanie Jeter and executive produced by Lilly Wachowski. Developed the visual sourcing, projector placement, throw-distance planning, testing, and on-set mapping workflow.

Overview

This project involved designing and executing practical projection mapping for a film set. I worked with the director and DP to source visuals, plan projector placement and throw distance, test projection options, and map the final imagery on set. The role sat between VFX, technical planning, and on-set collaboration, translating creative direction into a practical projection setup for production.

Credits

Directed by Stephanie Jeter; executive produced by Lilly Wachowski.
